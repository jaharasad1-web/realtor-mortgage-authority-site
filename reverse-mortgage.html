Signature Specialty

HECM for Purchase: retirement housing strategy, not just a loan product.

HUD describes HECM for Purchase as a way for eligible borrowers to purchase a new principal residence and obtain a HECM in a single transaction. Borrowers must meet FHA/HUD eligibility and counseling requirements and remain responsible for ongoing property charges and property maintenance.

The Basics

HECM education in plain language.

Age & Occupancy

HUD states HECM borrowers must be at least 62 and occupy the property as a principal residence, subject to program requirements.

HUD Counseling

Prospective HECM borrowers participate in counseling with a HUD-certified HECM counselor before the loan can proceed.

Property Responsibilities

Borrowers remain responsible for property taxes, homeowners insurance, maintenance and other applicable property charges.

Ownership

The borrower retains ownership of the home while the HECM remains in place, subject to the mortgage terms and obligations.

Family Questions

Adult children and heirs should understand repayment, sale and estate considerations before a family makes a decision.

Common Questions

Get clear before you decide.

Do I give up ownership of my home?

No. HUD states that the borrower retains ownership of the property while the HECM is in place, subject to the mortgage terms and borrower obligations.

Do HECM borrowers still have housing expenses?

Yes. Property taxes, homeowners insurance, maintenance and other applicable property charges remain the borrower's responsibility.

Can a reverse mortgage be used to buy a home?

Yes. FHA's HECM for Purchase program allows eligible borrowers to finance the purchase of a new principal residence and obtain a HECM in the same transaction.

Is counseling required?

HUD requires prospective HECM borrowers to participate in a counseling session with a HUD-certified HECM counselor.

Is a HECM right for everyone age 62 or older?

No. Age alone does not determine suitability or eligibility. Property, financial, counseling and underwriting requirements apply, and the strategy should fit the borrower's goals.

Source: HUD/FHA HECM program information. HUD Reverse Mortgage for Seniors.
