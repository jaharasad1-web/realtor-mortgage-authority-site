## Educational Post 1 — Home + Land
**Manufactured Home Question: Is the land included?**

That one answer can change how the transaction is structured.

A manufactured home on owned land can be very different from a home located in a leased-land community. Title, appraisal, financing and monthly obligations can all be affected.

Before making an offer, understand exactly what you are buying.

## Educational Post 2 — Foundation + Installation
**The home may look great—but does the installation meet the financing requirements?**

Depending on the loan program, permanent-foundation, installation, engineering and property-condition requirements may apply.

The earlier we identify those issues, the better positioned you are to avoid surprises later.

## Educational Post 3 — Financing Paths
**Can you finance a manufactured home with FHA, USDA, VA or Conventional financing?**

Possibly.

The answer depends on the borrower, the home, land, title, age, condition, installation, appraisal and specific loan-program requirements.

The right question is not just “Can I qualify?” It is “Does this entire transaction qualify?”

## Educational Post 4 — Before You Pay Deposits
**Before you put money down on a manufactured home, review the financing path first.**

Confirm:
- home age and HUD data
- title status
- land ownership
- foundation / installation
- occupancy requirements
- property eligibility
- lender/program requirements

This can help prevent a financing problem after you have already committed money.

## Compliance guardrails
Do not claim:
- every manufactured home qualifies for financing
- guaranteed FHA / USDA / VA / Conventional approval
- guaranteed appreciation or equity growth
- guaranteed low down payment
- guaranteed payment amount, rate or closing timeline
- that every property can be converted to real property or meet lender requirements

Use language such as:
- may qualify
- may be available
- subject to borrower and property eligibility
- subject to underwriting and approval
- program availability varies

Required public disclaimer concept:
Manufactured-home financing is subject to borrower, property, title, land, installation, appraisal, program and lender eligibility requirements. Program availability varies. All loans are subject to underwriting and approval.
