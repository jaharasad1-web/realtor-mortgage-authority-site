## 6. EDUCATIONAL ORGANIC POSTS

Post A — Myth interruption:
“Down payment assistance” does not mean one universal program or one guaranteed amount. Programs can have different income limits, purchase price limits, location rules, occupancy requirements, repayment terms and funding availability. The right question is: Which programs, if any, fit your situation?

Post B — Cash-to-close education:
Your down payment is not always the same as your total cash to close. Buyers may also need to plan for items such as closing costs, prepaid taxes and insurance, inspections, appraisal and reserves depending on the transaction. Build the whole plan before you shop.

Post C — DPA + first mortgage:
Down payment assistance usually works together with a first mortgage. That means the loan program, DPA program and property all have to work together. Start with the financing structure — not the house.

## 14. COMPLIANCE GUARDRAILS

Never state or imply:
- every buyer qualifies
- guaranteed assistance
- guaranteed approval
- a guaranteed dollar amount
- guaranteed zero down or zero cash to close
- a universal minimum credit score
- assistance is free money in all cases
- a program is available in every location
- a consumer is approved before actual underwriting/program review

Use language such as:
- may be available
- qualified buyers
- depending on eligibility
- program availability and terms vary
- assistance may have repayment, forgiveness, occupancy or other requirements

Mortgage advertising must remain consistent with lender/compliance requirements. Public geography remains NC • SC • GA • IL • FL only.
