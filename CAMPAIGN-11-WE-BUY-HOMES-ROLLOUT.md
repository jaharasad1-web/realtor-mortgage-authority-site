## Core Positioning
**Understand All Your Options Before You Sell.**

This campaign is education-first. “We Buy Homes” is not presented as a promise that Jahar, TRMM, or any third party will purchase every property. A cash or as-is sale is one possible route. The seller should compare price, timing, repairs, fees, convenience, certainty, and estimated net proceeds before choosing a strategy.

The campaign should speak to homeowners who may value speed or simplicity, including inherited properties, unwanted homes, homes needing major repairs, vacant properties, landlord exits, relocation situations, financial pressure, and owners who simply want to compare selling paths.

## Organic Educational Post 1 — Cash Offer vs. Traditional Listing
**A higher offer is not always the same as higher net proceeds—and a faster offer is not always the best overall deal.**

When comparing a cash/as-is sale with a traditional listing, look at:

• Gross price
• Repair expectations
• Seller concessions
• Commissions or service fees
• Closing costs
• Holding costs
• Timeline
• Certainty of closing
• Estimated net proceeds

The right choice depends on what matters most to you.

CTA: **Request a Home Sale Options Review.**

## Organic Educational Post 2 — Selling a Home That Needs Repairs
A home does not always need to be fully renovated before it can be sold.

Depending on the property and market, you may be able to:

• Sell as-is
• Make only selected repairs
• Prepare the home for the open market
• Explore an investor or cash-sale route

The best approach is the one that balances your expected sale proceeds, time, stress, repair budget and next move.

CTA: **Review my selling options.**

## Organic Educational Post 3 — Inherited / Unwanted Property
Inherited a home you do not plan to keep?

Before deciding, consider the condition of the property, ownership/title questions, carrying costs, family goals, market value, potential repairs, sale timing and tax/legal guidance where appropriate.

You do not have to choose a sale strategy before understanding the options.

CTA: **Request a Home Sale Options Review.**

## Organic Educational Post 4 — Landlord Exit
If you are tired of managing a rental property, the decision is bigger than “sell or keep.”

You may need to consider occupancy, lease terms, property condition, timing, market demand, financing on the property and the net proceeds from different selling paths.

CTA: **Compare my landlord-exit options.**

## Organic Educational Post 5 — Need a Faster Closing
A fast closing can be valuable when you are relocating, settling an estate, handling a vacant property, facing mounting carrying costs or simply need certainty.

But speed has a value—and sometimes that value is reflected in the price or terms offered.

Compare both the convenience and the financial outcome before deciding.

## Compliance + Ethical Guardrails
Do not state or imply:
- Jahar / TRMM will purchase every home
- a purchase offer is guaranteed
- a cash offer is automatically the best choice
- the seller will receive a specific price or net amount without analysis
- guaranteed closing date
- guaranteed foreclosure prevention
- guaranteed equity preservation
- no fees unless that is actually documented for a specific transaction
- every property qualifies for a direct or investor purchase

Use:
- “may be an option”
- “compare your options”
- “subject to property, market, buyer and transaction terms”
- “estimated net proceeds”
- “traditional listing, as-is sale or another strategy”
- “no purchase offer is guaranteed”

For homeowners under financial pressure or facing foreclosure, do not pressure them into a sale. Direct them to review all appropriate homeowner options, including contacting their mortgage servicer promptly and seeking qualified legal, tax or HUD-approved housing counseling guidance when appropriate.

## Role / Conflict Clarity
Because Jahar serves as a Realtor® and MLO, campaign language must remain clear about the service being offered. The consumer should not be led to believe a cash purchase is guaranteed or that accepting an investor/direct purchase route is required to receive real-estate or mortgage guidance.

If a third-party investor or direct-buyer route is introduced, disclose the nature of that relationship and any compensation or material interest as required by applicable law, brokerage policy and transaction circumstances.
