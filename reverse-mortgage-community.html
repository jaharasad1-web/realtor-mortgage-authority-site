Welcome to the Community

Welcome to the TRMM Reverse Mortgage & Retirement Housing Community™.

This community exists to help homeowners, families and professionals better understand retirement housing decisions through education, planning and trusted guidance.

We will discuss Reverse Mortgages, HECM for Purchase, rightsizing, aging in place, retirement relocation, helping aging parents, family conversations and related housing topics.

This is an education-first community. No pressure. No spam. Respectful questions are welcome.

Community Standards

Simple rules. Useful conversations.

1. Respect Everyone

Different families make different decisions. Disagreement is allowed; disrespect is not.

2. Education Before Promotion

No unsolicited sales pitches, spam or mass solicitation.

3. Protect Privacy

Do not post confidential personal, medical or financial information.

4. Ask Questions

The purpose of the community is learning. Questions are encouraged.
