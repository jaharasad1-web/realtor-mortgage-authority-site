What We Check First

0% Down Eligibility

Eligible USDA transactions can be financed without a required down payment, subject to program and underwriting requirements.

Area Eligibility

The property address must fall within a USDA-eligible geographic area.

Household Income

USDA income limits vary by location and household size, so household income is part of the qualification review.

Property Eligibility

The home must meet USDA property, appraisal and occupancy requirements and be used as a primary residence.

Homes USDA May Help Finance

The specific home, location, lender and transaction structure all matter.

Existing Homes

Eligible existing single-family homes may qualify when the property meets USDA requirements.

New Construction

Some new-construction transactions may qualify when USDA and lender requirements are satisfied.

Manufactured Homes

Eligible manufactured-home transactions may be possible when the home, land and installation meet applicable guidelines.

Rural + Suburban Areas

USDA eligibility can include many communities outside major urban cores—not only remote rural locations.
