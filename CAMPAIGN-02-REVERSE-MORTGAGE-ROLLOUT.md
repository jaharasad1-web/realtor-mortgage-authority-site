## Locked Positioning

Core message: Live Your Retirement on Your Terms.

Education-first framing:
- Start with the homeowner’s housing and retirement goals, not the product.
- Compare staying in the current home, rightsizing, relocating, traditional financing, selling, HECM, and HECM for Purchase when appropriate.
- Do not present a HECM as automatically right for every homeowner.
- Do not imply government endorsement.
- Do not describe proceeds, savings, qualification, rates, or payment outcomes as guaranteed.
- When discussing monthly payments, use the accurate phrase: “No required monthly principal-and-interest mortgage payment while loan terms are met.”
- Always preserve homeowner responsibilities: property taxes, homeowners insurance, applicable HOA/property charges, maintenance, occupancy requirements, and other loan obligations.
- HUD-approved counseling is required before a HECM can proceed.
