## Strategic Positioning

Condo financing is not only about whether the buyer qualifies. The condominium project itself can affect financing options.

The campaign should help consumers understand that a lender may review project-level characteristics in addition to the individual unit and borrower. The goal is to encourage an early project pre-screen before a buyer becomes committed to a particular condo.

Core education topics:

- warrantable vs. non-warrantable project considerations
- project eligibility and lender review
- occupancy type
- HOA/project characteristics
- insurance and budget considerations where applicable
- litigation, commercial space, investor concentration or other project factors where applicable
- conventional financing pathways
- alternative/non-warrantable options subject to actual program and lender guidelines
- primary residence and second-home financing conversations
- the importance of reviewing the project before making financing assumptions

Do not present project approval as guaranteed or universal.

## Organic Education Post 2 — Warrantable vs. Non-Warrantable

**What does “non-warrantable condo” mean?**

It does not automatically mean the condo cannot be financed.

It generally means the project may not meet the standard project requirements for a particular agency or conventional financing path.

Depending on the circumstances, another financing option may exist—but it must be reviewed against the actual borrower, project and lender guidelines.

CTA: **LET’S REVIEW THE PROJECT AND THE FINANCING TOGETHER.**

## Organic Education Post 3 — Before the Offer

Before you write an offer on a condo, ask early:

- What financing program are you planning to use?
- Has the project been reviewed?
- Are there known project-level issues?
- What HOA/project documents may be needed?
- Is your intended occupancy compatible with the financing path?

Early questions can reduce surprises later.

## Compliance Guardrails

Do not say or imply:

- every condo is financeable
- every project can be approved
- project pre-screen equals final project approval
- guaranteed financing
- guaranteed loan approval
- guaranteed rate, payment, down payment or closing cost
- every non-warrantable condo can be financed
- one lender’s or program’s project criteria automatically applies to all programs
- the consumer has been approved before actual underwriting

Use language such as:

- may qualify
- may be available
- project eligibility varies
- subject to program guidelines
- subject to lender and underwriting requirements
- based on borrower, property and project eligibility
- project pre-screen / project review

Do not use “approved condo” casually when only a preliminary or historical project lookup has been completed.
