DON’T COUNT YOURSELF OUT TOO EARLY

Common myths can keep qualified buyers from even checking their options.

MYTH: “I need 20% down.” FACT: Many mortgage programs require substantially less than 20%, and assistance may be available for eligible buyers.
MYTH: “My credit must be perfect.” FACT: Credit requirements vary by mortgage and assistance program. The right first step is a review, not an assumption.
MYTH: “Assistance is always free money.” FACT: DPA can be structured as grants, forgivable assistance, deferred loans, repayable seconds or other program-specific structures.

WHAT WE’LL CHECK TOGETHER

INCOME & HOUSEHOLD

CREDIT & DEBT-TO-INCOME

PRIMARY RESIDENCE

Many programs are designed for owner-occupied primary residences.

PROPERTY & LOCATION

Property type, purchase price and geography may affect available programs.

FIRST-TIME BUYER STATUS

Some programs use specific definitions; others may be open to repeat buyers.

EDUCATION & FUNDING

Education requirements and program funding availability can affect timing and eligibility.
