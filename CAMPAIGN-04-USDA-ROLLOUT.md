## CAMPAIGN POSITIONING

Core message:

**Homeownership May Be Closer Than You Think.**

USDA financing may provide 100% financing for eligible borrowers purchasing eligible properties in qualifying areas. The campaign must remain education-first and must never imply that every buyer, property, location or transaction qualifies.

The primary strategy is to help consumers understand that USDA eligibility depends on multiple factors, including:
- property location
- household income
- household size
- occupancy
- property type
- borrower qualification
- lender/underwriting requirements

Use “0% down” only with clear context that it means no required down payment for eligible USDA transactions. Closing costs, prepaid items and other transaction expenses may still apply.

**USDA MYTH: “I have to live in the middle of nowhere.”**

Not necessarily.

USDA eligibility is based on designated geographic areas, and some suburban and smaller-community locations may qualify.

The smartest move is not guessing. Check the actual address or area.

## EDUCATIONAL SOCIAL POST 2 — HOUSEHOLD INCOME

**USDA is not just about credit and payment. Household income matters too.**

USDA income limits vary by location and household size. That means the eligibility review should include everyone in the household whose income is counted under program rules.

Do the eligibility review before assuming you qualify—or assuming you do not.

## EDUCATIONAL SOCIAL POST 3 — PROPERTY TYPES

USDA financing may be considered for more than one type of home, depending on program and lender requirements.

Potential categories may include:
- eligible existing single-family homes
- certain new-construction transactions
- eligible manufactured-home transactions

The home, land, installation, location and transaction structure all matter.

## COMPLIANCE GUARDRAILS

Do not say:
- everyone qualifies
- guaranteed approval
- guaranteed 0% down
- no money needed at closing
- every rural/suburban property qualifies
- USDA has no income limits

Use:
- “may qualify”
- “eligible borrowers”
- “eligible properties”
- “qualifying areas”
- “subject to program and underwriting requirements”

Always clarify that 100% financing / 0% down refers to the absence of a required down payment on eligible transactions and does not mean that closing costs, prepaid items or other transaction expenses automatically disappear.
