Our Purpose

Homeownership is a journey—not a single transaction.

Buying, selling and financing a home are among the most important financial decisions many individuals and families will make. TRMM brings together real estate expertise, mortgage financing and education so people can understand their options before taking the next step.

Whether you're preparing for your first purchase, exploring USDA Rural Development financing, renovating a property, planning for retirement, building wealth through real estate or preparing to sell, the goal is the same: clear information, coordinated guidance and confidence in the decision.

“Every conversation should leave you more informed, more confident and better prepared to make your next homeownership decision.”

The TRMM Method

Meet Our Founder

Jahar Asad

Founder, TRMM — The Realtor & Mortgage Man™

Jahar Asad founded TRMM to help individuals and families make confident homeownership decisions by bringing education, real estate and mortgage financing together under one trusted resource.

Jahar is an Independent Mortgage Loan Officer and Certified Down Payment Assistance Specialist and Certified Reverse Mortgage Specialist with NEXA Lending. He is also a Realtor®, Nationally Certified Buyer Agent Expert and Strategic Pricing Agent with Moselle Realty Group Powered by Epique Realty.

His real estate and mortgage work is strengthened by years of corporate experience with Citigroup, where he served as a Real Estate Portfolio Supervisor. Before entering this chapter of his career, Jahar spent more than 25 years in mental health and retired as a Mental Health Team Leader serving children, adults and families.

Jahar earned a Bachelor of Public Administration from the illustrious HBCU Grambling State University. His background in public service, corporate real estate operations and mental health continues to shape a client-first approach rooted in education, listening and practical guidance.

Beyond The Office

Community, family and a life outside real estate.

Jahar is an avid fisherman, hunter, beekeeper and gardener. He loves to travel and has a goal of pursuing pilot lessons to fly general aviation aircraft. He has spearheaded food giveaways, assisted with community needs and believes strongly in giving back.

Jahar resides in Sophia, North Carolina, a rural community near Greensboro, with his wife Nafeesah. Together they have six children.
