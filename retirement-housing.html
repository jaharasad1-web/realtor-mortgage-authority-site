HECM for Purchase

Buying a home during retirement can look different.

HECM for Purchase Education

For eligible borrowers age 62 and older, a HECM for Purchase can be one way to purchase a new primary residence using a combination of borrower funds and a reverse mortgage. Borrowers remain responsible for property taxes, homeowners insurance, property maintenance and other applicable obligations.

TRMM focus: education first, lifestyle goals first, financing second.
