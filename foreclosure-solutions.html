PRE-FORECLOSURE OPTIONS

You Have Options. Let’s Review Them Early.

If you are behind on your mortgage or worried about foreclosure, the earlier you understand your choices, the more time you may have to act. The right path depends on your loan, servicer, equity, timeline, goals and eligibility.

Loan Modification

Ask your mortgage servicer whether a modification or other loss-mitigation option may be available based on your circumstances.

Sell Your Home

If selling makes sense, we can discuss traditional sale strategy, estimated equity and timing before a foreclosure date.

Temporary Relief

Forbearance, deferment or other servicer options may exist depending on your loan and hardship situation.

Short Sale

If you owe more than the home may sell for, a short sale may be worth discussing with the servicer and qualified advisers.

Investor / As-Is Sale

Some homeowners may prefer a faster as-is sale. We can compare that route with a traditional listing so you can evaluate the tradeoffs.

Transition Planning

We can help you think through timing, next housing steps and how to protect as much remaining equity as possible.
