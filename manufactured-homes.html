Financing Paths We Can Review

The right loan depends on the home, how it is titled, the land, installation, age, condition, occupancy and borrower qualifications.

FHA

Eligible manufactured-home transactions may qualify under FHA programs when program and property requirements are met.

USDA

USDA may provide low- or no-down-payment options for eligible borrowers, homes and geographic areas.

VA

Qualified veterans and service members may have manufactured-home financing options when lender and VA requirements are met.

Conventional

Conventional financing may be available for qualifying manufactured-home transactions that meet agency and lender guidelines.

What We Check Before You Fall in Love With the Home

Home + HUD Data

Year built, HUD labels/data plate, size, condition and whether the home meets the intended loan program.

Land + Title

Whether land is included, how the home is titled, and whether the transaction is real property or another structure.

Foundation + Installation

Permanent foundation, installation and engineering requirements may apply depending on the program.

Location + Use

Primary-residence requirements, community rules, zoning and geographic eligibility can affect financing.
