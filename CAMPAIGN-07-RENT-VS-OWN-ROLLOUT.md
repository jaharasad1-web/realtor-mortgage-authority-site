## Educational post 1 — Monthly payment is not enough
A rent-vs-own comparison should include more than the rent payment and mortgage principal + interest.

For ownership, also consider taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance if applicable, HOA dues, maintenance, utilities, closing costs and reserves.

For renting, consider future rent increases, renewal fees, flexibility and how long you expect to remain in the property.

CTA: **Compare the full picture.**

## Educational post 2 — Equity
Ownership may allow a borrower to reduce principal and build equity over time, but property appreciation is never guaranteed.

That means equity should be part of the conversation—not a promise.

CTA: **See what ownership could look like for you.**

## Educational post 3 — Rent increases
Your current rent is only one part of the decision.

Ask:
- What is my rent today?
- What could renewal look like next year?
- How long do I expect to remain a renter?
- What amount am I already comfortable paying each month?

Then compare those numbers with a realistic homeownership scenario.

## Educational post 4 — Financing paths
Depending on borrower and property eligibility, a homebuyer may have access to FHA, Conventional, USDA or VA financing, plus eligible down-payment-assistance options.

Do not advertise any program as guaranteed. Qualification, program rules, property eligibility and underwriting apply.

## Compliance guardrails
- Do not state or imply that buying is always cheaper than renting.
- Do not guarantee appreciation, equity growth, refinance opportunities or future rates.
- Do not guarantee approval, loan program eligibility or DPA eligibility.
- Distinguish modeled payments from actual loan quotes.
- Include taxes, insurance, maintenance, HOA and mortgage insurance in ownership discussions when applicable.
- Financing is subject to borrower, property, program and underwriting requirements.
- Keep Equal Housing Opportunity / Equal Housing Lender language where applicable.
- Avoid targeting or excluding protected classes.
