## Conversion Strategy

The campaign intentionally uses two different conversion paths:

1. Talk to Jahar now — direct First-Time Homebuyer Plan.
2. Learn from Jahar first — Homebuying 101™ MasterClass.

Do not treat webinar registration as the same stage as a preapproval or active buyer inquiry.

Suggested CRM lifecycle:
Registered → Attended → No-Show → Strategy Session → Mortgage Review / Preapproval → Buyer Consultation → Active Home Search → Under Contract → Closed
