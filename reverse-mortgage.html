Live Your Retirement on Your Terms.

For eligible homeowners age 62+, a federally insured Home Equity Conversion Mortgage (HECM) may allow access to a portion of home equity without a required monthly principal-and-interest mortgage payment while loan terms are met. The right strategy starts with your goals—not the product.

Supplement Retirement Cash Flow

Explore whether available HECM proceeds could support day-to-day retirement needs while preserving other resources.

Healthcare + Home Improvements

Evaluate whether equity access could help with healthcare expenses, accessibility improvements, repairs or aging-in-place goals.

Stay in the Home You Know

For homeowners who want to remain in place, compare a HECM with other housing, financing and downsizing options.

Reduce Existing Obligations

A HECM may be used to pay off an existing eligible mortgage at closing and could change monthly cash-flow demands.

Flexible Proceeds Options

Depending on eligibility and loan structure, proceeds may be available through different disbursement methods. Not every option fits every borrower.

Family + Estate Conversation

Understand how the loan balance, future home sale and heirs may be affected before deciding whether a HECM aligns with your broader plan.

What remains your responsibility: property taxes, homeowners insurance, applicable HOA or property charges, maintenance, occupancy requirements and other loan obligations. HUD-approved counseling is required before a HECM can proceed.
